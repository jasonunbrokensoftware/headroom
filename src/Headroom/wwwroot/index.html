How this estimate works

One unit is the full weekly Codex allowance on Pro 100. A full Plus allowance is 0.1 units; Pro 100 is 1; Pro 200 is 2; Pro 500 is 5. Each account’s remaining fraction is multiplied by its plan’s capacity. Totals cover the main Codex weekly allowance; separate model allowances remain on the account cards.

Banked resets add potential capacity: use the existing allowance first, then redeem each reset before it expires. Redeeming early discards unused allowance. Scheduled future renewals are not added, and 5-hour and weekly limits are never counted twice.

Only current readings and verified, unexpired full-reset details count. Unknown plans and reset types are excluded. Purchased credits stay separate. Availability is still subject to each account’s usage limits. These are allowance equivalents, not calendar weeks or promises of tokens, tasks, or hours.

Estimates use published plan ratios from November 8, 2026, reviewed November 11, 2026. Actual allowances can change; these ratios are not live account entitlements.
