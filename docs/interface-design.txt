HEADROOM INTERFACE STUDY — NOVEMBER 11, 2026

Branch: design/interface-refresh (local experiment; main is unchanged).

Compared three coded layout prototypes using the same three example accounts,
long email, capacity totals, and forecast, at a 1200 x 780 viewport. Evaluated
headlessly through DOM bounds, without controlling or capturing the desktop.

Gallery: expanded cards, large capacity and forecast panels. Last account
bottom at 916 px. Familiar, but repeats labels and reserves too much space
for empty resets and secondary actions.

Sidebar: persistent navigation and vertically stacked insights. Last account
bottom at 1216 px. Gives future sections room, but consumes useful account
width and pushes essential balances below the fold.

Focus: capacity and runway side by side, aligned account rows, separate Activity
view. Last account bottom at 832 px in the rough prototype, with room to tighten
spacing in implementation. No email clipping or horizontal page overflow.

Selected direction: Focus. Keep capacity and runway immediately visible, put
account identity, allowance, reset time, and banked resets into predictable
columns, and move destructive actions into account menus. Keep information
about calculations available in disclosures. Activity gets dedicated space
for scenario controls and graphs without crowding everyday monitoring.

Visual direction: charcoal surfaces, restrained periwinkle for emphasis,
mint for banked capacity, amber for limits and stale data. Tabular numbers,
one spacing scale, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and compact
loading/empty/error states. Account colors stay consistent across filtering.

The custom title bar, controls, and frame are drawn by the app. Windows keeps
native move/resize/snap behavior, but must not draw its title bar or border.
No additional frontend dependencies or network fonts are introduced.

Implemented layout: at 1200 x 780, the third account ends at 721 px. Both
capacity and runway summaries and all three account rows fit without scrolling.
Validated at 520, 680, 800, 1024, 1200, 1440, and 2560 px: consistent section edges,
long emails on one line, no horizontal overflow, SVG labels inside their plots,
and captions separated from axis text. Smaller windows use stacked account
sections; the 520 px case also covers a small window under display scaling.
Larger account sets remain searchable and scroll vertically.

The Overview always summarizes all accounts at recent pace, including verified
banked resets. Activity account filters and scenario controls remain independent.
Account menus support Escape and restore focus; refresh remains named for screen
readers when its text is hidden in small windows. History failure has an explicit
retry state instead of an endless loading animation.

Frame implementation references:
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/dwmapi/ne-dwmapi-dwmwindowattribute
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/dwmapi/ne-dwmapi-dwmncrenderingpolicy
WM_NCCALCSIZE continues to provide the full client area. DWM non-client rendering
is disabled, and Windows 11's separate border color is set to DWMWA_COLOR_NONE.
Native interactions still require manual verification; no desktop control or
screen capture was used during this redesign.
